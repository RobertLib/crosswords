A calm word game with no ads and nothing to buy. Swipe across the letters on the wheel, spell words and watch them fill the crossword, then travel on through 24 landscapes, from a morning meadow past the northern lights to the summit of light.

NO ADS. NOTHING TO BUY. NO WI-FI NEEDED.
• No ads between levels, or anywhere else
• No in-app purchases and no subscriptions: coins are earned by playing, never sold
• Plays completely offline, because every puzzle is inside the app
• No account, no tracking, no data collected

1,200 LEVELS IN 100 CHAPTERS
Chapter by chapter, 12 levels at a time, you travel through 24 landscapes, with drifting clouds and falling petals, fireflies, snow or the aurora. Puzzles start with three letters and grow to seven, and every chapter builds to a finale on its biggest wheel.

A NEW CROSSWORD EVERY DAY
Solve the daily puzzle to grow your streak, see every solved day in the calendar and earn bonus coins for every 7 days in a row. An optional reminder nudges you when today's puzzle is still waiting, and stays quiet once it's solved.

EVERY REAL WORD COUNTS
Words the wheel can spell that aren't in the crossword still count as bonus words, and every ten of them opens a gift of coins. Every word you find is kept in your Lexicon, where you can look up what it means in your device's dictionary.

HINTS YOU EARN
Stuck? Light Bulb reveals the next letter of a word, Spotlight any square you choose and Starburst five letters across the grid. Hints cost coins, and coins only come from playing. Solve a level without hints and it earns a crown.

PLAY AT YOUR OWN PACE
No countdown, no lives, no energy to wait for. Leave in the middle of a level and the puzzle waits exactly where you left it. 12 achievements, each with bronze, silver, gold and platinum tiers, and statistics of everything you've found.

ENGLISH AND CZECH
1,200 levels and a daily puzzle in each language. The game follows your device's language, and you can switch the puzzles in Settings; each language keeps its own journey and lexicon. Offensive words never appear in the crossword.

FOR EVERYONE
VoiceOver and Switch Control support, letters you can tap one by one instead of swiping, a spoken summary of the crossword, Reduce Motion throughout and Dynamic Type in the menus. On iPhone and iPad.
