# Privacy Policy — Wordwander

*Last updated: November 2, 2026*

## In short

Wordwander does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

## In detail

**We collect no data.** The game never asks for your name, email address, phone
number or any other personal information. No account and no sign-in is required.

**It does not connect to the internet.** Wordwander contains no networking code.
Every puzzle and every word list, in English and in Czech, is inside the app; the
daily puzzle is picked from the date on your device; and the sounds are generated
on the device. Everything works entirely offline.

**No analytics, no advertising.** The app contains no ad networks, no analytics
frameworks and no third-party libraries that transmit anything anywhere. It links
only Apple's own frameworks.

**No in-app purchases.** The app contains no in-app purchases and no
subscriptions. The coins you spend on hints are earned by playing and cannot be
bought.

**Where your progress is stored.** Your levels, coins, streaks, achievements, the
words in your lexicon and your settings are stored solely on your device, in one
file in the app's own storage. None of this ever leaves the device. Your progress
is included in your regular device backup (iCloud or computer) if you have one
enabled, in which case Apple's own policies apply. Deleting the app removes this
data from your device.

**Daily reminder.** If you turn on the daily reminder in Settings, the app asks
for permission to show notifications and then schedules them on your device. They
are local notifications: no server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere. The
reminder is off until you turn it on.

**Word definitions.** The lexicon shows definitions from the dictionaries
installed on your device, through iOS's own dictionary viewer. The app itself
sends nothing anywhere when you look a word up.

**Device access.** The app does not use the camera, microphone, location
services, contacts, photos, health data or any other sensitive permission. It
only uses haptic feedback, audio playback and, if you turn on the reminder,
notifications.

**Children.** The game is rated 4+ and is suitable for any age. Because the app
collects no data, no data about anyone of any age is created.

## Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
