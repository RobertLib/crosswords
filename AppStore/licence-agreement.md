# Licence agreement (EULA)

Every App Store app comes with Apple's [Licensed Application End User License Agreement](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/)
unless the developer gives it one of its own. That standard agreement forbids copying,
modifying or redistributing the app. The puzzle packs inside Wordwander are under CC BY-SA 4.0
(see [LICENSE.md](../LICENSE.md)), and section 2(a)(5)(C) of that licence forbids offering or
imposing additional terms that restrict its rights. The packs are published in the public
repository, but the agreement that ships with the app should not contradict that licence
either, so the app gets its own: Apple's standard text followed by the clause below.

This is the usual approach for App Store apps with openly licensed content, but it is not legal
advice. If the app ever goes beyond a free download (in-app purchases, subscriptions), read the
whole agreement again.

## In App Store Connect

1. Open the app, then **App Information** → **License Agreement** → **Edit**.
2. Choose to apply a custom licence agreement to all countries or regions.
3. Paste the current text of Apple's standard agreement (link above), followed by the clause
   below.
4. Save. The agreement applies from the next version submitted for review.

When Apple updates its standard agreement, a custom one does not follow. Paste the new text again
with the clause after it.

> agreement restricts the rights that licence grants you in that content, and as to that content,
> that licence prevails over any conflicting term of this agreement. The rest of the Licensed
> Application is licensed under the terms above.

Keep the repository address in the clause in step with the one in Settings › Acknowledgements and
in `LICENSE.md`.
